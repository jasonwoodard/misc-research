The Cost Curve

Tin Can is cheapest through month 27. Build-it-yourself is cheapest after.

  • Year 1 total: Tin Can + Party Line $210. Build it yourself $271. MyPhone $277.
  • Tin Can, no subscription: $100 total, forever — cheapest of all four, Can2Can calling only.
  • Month 11: build-it-yourself total cost drops below MyPhone's.
  • Month 27: build-it-yourself total cost drops below Tin Can + Party Line's.
  • Tin Can + Party Line vs. MyPhone: no crossover. Tin Can is cheaper at every month.
4

Can2Can Mechanics

A build-it-yourself or MyPhone line cannot reach a Tin Can with no subscription — and can't be reached by one either.

Every Tin Can is assigned a real phone number at setup, free tier included. The number existing isn't restricted — the routing is.
